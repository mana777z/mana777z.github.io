Feature Introduction

◇Images are not saved in "Photos (Camera Roll)"
Images are saved only within the app. This eliminates the problem of images getting buried among other photos and causing you to wonder, "Where did that image go?" or "I don't want to back up this image..."
◇Sort by due date
You can save your schedule's planned dates and short notes. You can sort them by planned date.You can also view them anytime using the widget.
◇Compatible with "Apple Intelligence"
On compatible devices, Apple Intelligence automatically retrieves the scheduled date and a short memo.
◇Save all the text in the image as tags
The OCR function saves all the text on a document. Combined with the search function, it can find even words you only vaguely remember, such as "I think it was a document from school? It said something like XX" or "Hmm, what was that thing in XX town called...?"
◇Supports iPhone's sharing function
In addition to "taking a photo with the camera" and "selecting from photos (camera roll)," this app also supports iPhone's sharing function. Images sent via mail, documents sent from school (PDF compatible), etc., can be sent to this app using the sharing function and registered immediately. You can also send images via email or LINE from this app, or print them using a printer.
